Agentic framework selection — four scenarios mapped to recommended frameworks with rationale
Scenario	Framework	Why this framework?
Scenario 1
The understaffed marketing team
CrewAI	BBest for structured multi-agent handoffs — low-code/no-code, quick to launch, and easy to tweak later without writing code.
Scenario 2
The research-brief assembly line
CrewAI	Best for structured multi-agent handoffs — define clear roles like researcher, analyst, writer, and editor without building orchestration from scratch.
Scenario 3
The self-debugging data analyst
AutoGen	Best for open-ended multi-agent conversations — coder and critic agents iteratively talk, execute code, inspect errors, and retry for an unknown number of turns.
Scenario 4
The regulated enterprise platform
LangChain + LangGraph	Best for production-grade enterprise systems — supports custom branching, checkpoints, pause/resume, human-in-the-loop, RAG, and strong observability.
